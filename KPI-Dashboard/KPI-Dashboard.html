Upload each source separately below (.xlsx / .xls / .csv). Once a source is uploaded, the dashboard will populate with your real data for that source. Other sources remain empty until their files are uploaded.

① Primary Sales

Billing-level sales from SAP. Drives the Main Channel / Sub Channel grouping across the whole dashboard.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Billing Date, Main Channel, Sub Channel, Region, Division, SKU Code, SKU, Sales Person, Qty, Sales Value, Profit

② Debtor Data Table

Open-item ageing per debtor. Drives Over-60-Days Debtors % and Avg Collection Period.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Document Date, Amount in Local Currency, Channel, <7D, 7D-13D, 14D-30D, 31D-60D, 61D-90D, 91D-120D, 121D-180D, 181D-360D, 361D+

③ Inventory Snapshot

Stock value/qty by ageing bucket. Drives Over-90-Days Stock % and stock ageing chart. No channel field — company-wide only.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Division, Material Description, 0 - 30 Days Value, 30 - 60 Days Value, 60 - 90 Days Value, Over 90 Days Value, Total Value, Total Qty, Time Period

④ Sales & Returns (Axianta)

Secondary sales / free issues / returns from the field. No channel field — grouped by Region/Territory/Brand.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Date, TypeTxn, Region, Territory, Brand, OutletType, GrossValue, NetValue, Qty, TotalFreeUnits

⑤ Cost of Sales / Overheads

Material cost, labour, electricity, machine & overhead absorption, royalty. Drives the full Cost-of-Sales breakdown panel and Avg Stock Holding Period / Avg Settlement Period.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Time Period, Material Cost, 20:1 FOC, VAT for NBT2%, Damage Good, Production consumable, Labour, Labour Absorb, Electricity, Building Maintain, Consumables, Machinery & Tool Maintenance, Overhead-Other Labour, Overhead-Other Machine, Machine, Machine Absorb, ROYALTY, Creditor Balance

⑥ Expenses & P&L Adjustments

Advertising, distribution cost, admin, donations, finance cost, bad debt / inventory adjustments, other income, corporate tax, majority interest, EVA. Drives the full P&L waterfall down to Net Profit After Tax.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Time Period, Advertising & Promotions MD, Advertising & Promotions, R&D Expenses, Distribution Cost, Employee Related, Donations, Administration, Shared Services, Finance Cost, Bad Debt, Inventory Adjustment, Other Income, Corporate Tax %, Majority Interest, EVA

⑦ Company Reference Metrics

Headcount, distributor count, and the "New KPIs" section (SS / LED / LVS quantities, customer complaints). Drives all Per-Head and Per-Distributor productivity KPIs.

Not Connected
Expected columns
Time Period, No. of Employees, No. of Manu Persons, No. of Distributors, SS Qty, LED Qty, LVS Qty, No of Customer Complaints
